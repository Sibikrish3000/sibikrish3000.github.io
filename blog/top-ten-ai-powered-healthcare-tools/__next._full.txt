20:Te75,### 1. Glass Health

* **Website:** [glass.health](https://glass.health)
* **Pricing:** Paid tool

Glass Health offers an AI system that supports doctors while they make clinical decisions. It helps draft possible diagnoses, treatment plans, and answers to common medical questions. It doesn’t handle medical images. It saves doctors time and helps them focus on patient care.

### 2. DeepLabCut

* **Website:** [deeplabcut.org](https://deeplabcut.org)
* **Pricing:** Free and open source

DeepLabCut started as a way to track how animals move but now helps analyze how people move too. It’s used in rehab, sports, and brain research. No special markers or suits are needed — it watches and measures motion directly, allowing researchers to shape it to their needs.

***

### 3. Ada Health

* **Website:** [ada.com/app](https://ada.com/app)
* **Pricing:** Free version available; deeper features via paid clinic partnerships

Ada is an AI symptom checker that helps people figure out what might be wrong before they see a doctor. It makes it easier for patients to understand their health and for doctors to prepare for visits.

***

### 4. Lingo AI (Abbott)

* **Website:** [hellolingo.com](https://hellolingo.com)
* **Pricing:** Paid subscription (device and app)

Lingo is a wearable device from Abbott that tracks things like glucose, ketones, lactate, or alcohol in real time. Users get instant feedback on how their body responds to food or exercise, giving people more control over daily health choices.

***

### 5. PathAI

* **Website:** [pathai.com](https://pathai.com)
* **Pricing:** Paid service for hospitals and labs

PathAI uses AI to help pathologists diagnose diseases, mainly cancer, more accurately. It aims to cut errors and speed up lab work by catching details that human eyes might miss, ultimately improving treatment.

***

### 6. [Paige.AI](https://Paige.AI)

* **Website:** [paige.ai](https://paige.ai)
* **Pricing:** Paid tools for labs and hospitals

[Paige.AI](https://Paige.AI) brings AI into cancer diagnostics. It highlights problem areas in pathology slides, helping pathologists make better calls. Its technology aims to boost precision and speed in cancer detection.

***

### 7. Headspace Health

* **Website:** [headspace.com](https://headspace.com)
* **Pricing:** Paid service

Headspace Health provides mental health support through an app that combines AI with real therapists. People can access therapy sessions, text support, and self-care tools. AI helps spot when someone might need extra help to make mental healthcare more reachable.
